# Filters

CUREUM has two voice filters (every note has its own pair) plus a filter in the FX rack. Click **FILTER 1** or **FILTER 2** in the panel header to edit a filter; the power buttons next to the names switch them on.

## Controls

- **TYPE**: the filter model. Types are grouped in the menu: Normal, Multi, Flanges, Misc and CUREUM.
- **CUTOFF**: the cutoff (or tuning) frequency. Drag horizontally on the response display to move it.
- **RES**: resonance. Drag vertically on the display. Many types self-oscillate at high values.
- **DRIVE**: saturation before or inside the filter.
- **VAR**: a type-specific control. Its label changes with the type: *Fat* (bass compensation), *Morph* (crossfades the responses of Multi types), *Damp*, *Spread*, *Vowel*, *Gain* and so on.
- **MIX**: dry/wet. **STEREO**: moves the left and right cutoff in opposite directions. **LEVEL**: output level. **KEYTRK**: makes the cutoff follow the keyboard.

## Routing

The row at the bottom of the panel sets where each source goes: **A, B, C** (oscillators), **N** (noise) and **S** (sub) can feed Filter 1, Filter 2, both, or go Direct. The last menu selects **Serial** (Filter 1 feeds Filter 2) or **Parallel** (both filters side by side).

## Filter families

- **MG Low 6-24**: a resonant transistor-ladder low-pass with musical saturation. MG Low 12 is the default.
- **Low / High / Band / Peak / Notch 6-24**: clean state-variable filters with 6 to 24 dB/octave slopes.
- **Multi (LH, LB, LP ... B/P/N)**: morphing filters. VAR fades between the responses named in the type (L = low-pass, H = high-pass, B = band-pass, P = peak, N = notch).
- **Cmb +/-, Flg +/-**: comb and flanger filters tuned to the cutoff. Positive and negative versions differ in their harmonic series; L6/H6 versions add damping.
- **Phs 12-120 and FPhs**: phaser-style allpass chains; VAR spreads the notches.
- **Low/Band/High EQ**: shelves and bells; VAR is the gain (50% = 0 dB) and RES the bandwidth.
- **Ring Mod, SampHold**: ring modulation at the cutoff frequency and sample-and-hold downsampling.
- **Combs, Allpasses, Reverb**: resonators and diffusers for metallic and roomy colours.
- **French LP, German LP, Diode LP, MS20 LP/HP, MG High 24**: characterful ladder and Sallen-Key style designs.
- **Formant I-III, Vowel Morph**: vocal formants; VAR sweeps the vowel (A-E-I-O-U).
- **Dist.Comb, Scream**: filters with distortion in the feedback path for aggressive sounds.
