# Sub and noise oscillators

## Sub oscillator

A simple, clean oscillator to add weight below the main oscillators.

- **Shape**: Sine, Round Rect, Triangle, Saw, Square or Pulse.
- **OCT / SEM**: pitch relative to the played note.
- **LEVEL / PAN**.
- **TO**: where the sub goes: Filter 1, Filter 2, both, or Direct (bypassing the filters, which keeps the low end clean).

## Noise oscillator

Plays a noise or texture sample. CUREUM includes coloured noises (white, pink, brown, blue, violet, grey), textures (vinyl, tape hiss, crackle, wind, rain, static, metallic, breath), mains hum and one-shot transients (click, thump, snap). **Load sample...** in the type menu lets you play any audio file.

- **PITCH**: playback rate; 50% is the original speed.
- **FINE**: fine rate adjustment.
- **KEY**: makes the playback rate follow the keyboard.
- **1-SHOT**: plays the sample once instead of looping, perfect for attack transients.
- **ST**: stereo noise (left and right decorrelated).
- **LEVEL / PAN / TO**: as for the sub oscillator.

Tip: route a short one-shot noise (Click or Snap) with a fast envelope to add bite to plucks and basses.
