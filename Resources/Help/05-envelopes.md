# Envelopes

CUREUM has four AHDSR envelopes. **Env 1 always controls the amplitude of every voice**; the other three are free modulation sources.

- **ATTACK**: rise time from 0 to full level.
- **HOLD**: time at full level before the decay.
- **DECAY**: fall time to the sustain level.
- **SUSTAIN**: level while the key is held.
- **RELEASE**: fall time after the key is released.
- **A/D/R CURVE**: the shape of each segment (0 = straight; positive values are exponential-like).

The display is editable: drag the round handles to change the times (and the sustain level), and drag the small open handles in the middle of the segments to bend them. The white dot shows the newest voice playing through the envelope.

To use an envelope as a modulator, drag its tab (ENV 2, ENV 3, ENV 4) onto a knob. A typical filter envelope: drag ENV 2 onto Filter 1 CUTOFF, set a short decay and a low sustain.
